📌 About the Project

Dey Fried Chicken is a restaurant website designed to introduce the brand, showcase its menu, and help customers access branch information and online ordering options. The website also highlights the restaurant's customer loyalty program and services.

✨ Features

- Homepage: Introduces the restaurant and its services.
- Online Ordering: Provides access to online food ordering.
- Digital Menu: Allows visitors to explore the restaurant's menu.
- Branch Locations: Displays information about restaurant branches.
- Customer Loyalty Program: Introduces membership benefits and promotional offers.
- About Us: Shares information about the restaurant and its commitment to food quality.
- Contact Information: Provides branch contact details and opening hours.
- Responsive Experience: Designed for convenient browsing across devices.
